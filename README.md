Build the GoPiGo Robot
Reference: Official Build Guide
A) Stage 1 — Chassis & Motors
1. Orient the base plate: the side marked up must face upward.
2. Bag names decoded:
o “2 Metal Brackets” = Caster Hardware
o “Hardware from the large bag” = Motor Hardware
3. Mount motors in holes labeled L and R (not “1”).
4. Part sources:
o Step 13 → Controller Hardware
o Step 16 → Acrylic Hardware
5. Standoffs: small silver posts → holes “2”; long silver posts → “1”.
6. Step 20 uses parts from Motor Hardware; continue to motor assembly.
B) Stage 2 — Board, Wheels, Battery
1. Skip the camera
2. Hardware bag references:
o Step 5 → Caster Hardware
o Step 8 spacers → Motor Hardware
o Step 11 gold posts → Controller Hardware
o Step 12 mini screws → Controller Hardware
o Step 15 → Acrylic Hardware
3. Steps 17–19: Plug battery pack directly into the GoPiGo3 board. With
rechargeable pack, you don’t need the 8×AA holder.
4. Mount the battery pack on top for stability and smoother driving.
5. Proceed to the tutorial’s CONNECT step.
C) First Power-On & Quick Check
• Turn on the power.
• If the power LED flashes endlessly or the antenna LED never turns green,
reflash the SD card with the official OS.
• Open Dashboard → DRIVE and test forward/back/turn briefly.
